2026 FDI World Dental Congress Event

Duluth, Minnesota

September 4-7th, 2026


Each year, FDI World Dental Congress (WDC) provides a unique platform for dental professionals to come together for four days of collaboration, education, and inspiration. In 2026, we are proud to host this prestigious event in Duluth, Minnesota, a region steeped in history, culture, and breathtaking views.

As we gather against the stunning background of Lake Superior, we look forward to an enlightening journey into the latest advancements in dentistry. This Congress will feature a cutting-edge scientific programme, offering a wealth of continuing education (CE) accredited sessions and engaging interactive forums on a range of dentistry-related topics. Moreover, attendees will get the opportunity to discover the latest innovations in the field at the expansive industry exhibition.

Whether you are a practitioner, researcher, student, industry representative, or a part of the dental team, we cannot wait to welcome you for the 2026 FDI WDC in Duluth. This is a great platform to foster connections, share knowledge, and shape the future of oral health and dentistry. Together let’s seize this unparalleled opportunity to learn, network and innovate.
